What can ScamShield check?

Identify scam and phishing patterns across your digital messages.

💬

Suspicious messages

SMS, WhatsApp, emails, and online chats pretending to be banks or services.

🔗

Suspicious links

Deceptive URLs, fake login pages, and link structures used by scammers.

🔑

Requests for OTPs or passwords

Attempts to steal verification codes, PINs, or account credentials.

💳

Urgent payment requests

Demands for immediate transfers, fake electricity bills, or tax payments.

🏛️

Fake authority messages

Impersonation of Police, CBI, Customs, Tax officials, or court summons.

⚠️

Social-engineering attempts

Pressure tactics, artificial urgency, and threats of immediate arrest or blocking.

    About ScamShield

    ScamShield is an AI-powered tool designed to help people identify suspicious digital communications.

    What ScamShield checks

    ✓
    Message content

    Evaluates words, phrasing, and vocabulary patterns commonly found in scam campaigns.

    ✓
    Links and URLs

    Examines link structures and deceptive domain techniques without executing harmful code.

    ✓
    Social-engineering signals

    Detects artificial urgency, authority impersonation, panic creation, and threats.

    ✓
    Suspicious requests

    Flags solicitations for passwords, OTPs, PINs, remote access, or rapid money transfers.

    ℹ️

    Important Safety Notice

    ScamShield is an assistance tool. Always verify important messages through official channels such as bank branches, verified websites, or certified customer support numbers.
